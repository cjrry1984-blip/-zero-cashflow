A few useful distinctions.

Is the calculator free?

Yes. You can use it and copy your estimate without signing up or entering an email address. The downloadable products linked below it are paid.

Does “time value” mean money I will earn?

No. It values time at the hourly rate you enter. Real savings depend on whether time is actually freed, how you use it and what the automation costs. Use the result to compare ideas, not to promise revenue.

Will these tools automate my business for me?

No. The calculator estimates value. The paid tools help with planning, drafting, reporting and tracking. They do not connect business accounts, run integrations or include an AI subscription.

What happens when I buy a toolkit?

Payhip handles checkout and download delivery. Unzip your download, read its guide and open index.html in a modern browser. You do not need a paid API to run the local tools.
