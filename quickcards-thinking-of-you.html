Thinking-of-you messages for any day

A thoughtful note does not need a special event. Sometimes the best reason to write is simply that somebody came to mind.

Three messages you can use

Example 1

No big reason — just wanted to say you're appreciated.

Example 2

You crossed my mind today, and I hope you're doing well.

Example 3

Sending a little good thought your way, just because.

Make it personal

Use the examples as a starting point, then add one detail that would make sense to the recipient.

  • Choose a line that matches how close you are.
  • You don't need to explain every detail of why you're writing.
  • Send a small, low-pressure message rather than asking for an immediate reply.

Before sending, read your message out loud. If it doesn't sound like you, simplify it.
