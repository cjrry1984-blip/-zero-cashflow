# Affiliate Disclosure and Editorial Policy

ZERO CASHFLOW is an independent AI tools and automation directory.

## Current commercial status
The published tool links are ordinary provider links. No affiliate partnership or commission is claimed. Affiliate links may be activated only after a provider has accepted the publisher and issued an approved tracking link.

## Disclosure placement
If affiliate links are activated, display a clear disclosure close to the relevant link and on the page explaining that ZERO CASHFLOW may earn a commission at no additional cost to the visitor. Sponsored placements must be labelled separately from editorial listings.

## Editorial rules
- Include tools because they solve a defined user problem, not solely because a commission is available.
- Verify features, prices, limits and terms against provider sources before publication.
- Date-stamp material reviews and provide a correction route.
- Never fabricate reviews, rankings, partnerships or performance claims.
- Do not use deceptive redirects, cookie stuffing, forced clicks or unsolicited bulk messages.
- Remove broken, expired or non-compliant affiliate links.
- Keep a record of programme terms, link status and review date.

## Launch checklist
1. Confirm publisher identity and business/tax setup.
2. Apply to the provider's official programme.
3. Wait for written acceptance.
4. Read programme rules and permitted traffic sources.
5. Create the tracked link in the approved dashboard.
6. Add a disclosure beside the link.
7. Test the link and record the date checked.
