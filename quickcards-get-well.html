Gentle get-well card messages

When someone is unwell, avoid pressure to feel better quickly. A kind message can simply offer comfort and let them know you are thinking of them.

Three messages you can use

Example 1

Thinking of you and sending gentle good wishes.

Example 2

There's no rush. Take care of yourself, one day at a time.

Example 3

I hope today brings a little comfort and an easier moment.

Make it personal

Use the examples as a starting point, then add one detail that would make sense to the recipient.

  • Avoid giving medical advice unless asked.
  • Don't promise a quick recovery when the situation is uncertain.
  • Offer specific practical help only when you can actually provide it.

Before sending, read your message out loud. If it doesn't sound like you, simplify it.
