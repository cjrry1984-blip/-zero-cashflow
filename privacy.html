Privacy

This static website does not run an account system, collect form submissions, or send saved recipe choices to a server. If you choose to save recipes, the site stores their recipe IDs in your browser's local storage on this device. This is used only to show your saved list and is not transmitted by this site.

The site does not intentionally use analytics or advertising cookies. The hosting provider may process technical request data to deliver the website. External links lead to third-party services that have their own privacy policies and terms.

Do not enter confidential, personal, client, health, financial or other sensitive information into third-party AI tools unless you have checked their terms and have a lawful basis to do so. Recipe instructions are general educational information, not legal, financial, security or professional advice.

IZZOPAC free calculator: optional statistics

The automation calculator only loads GoatCounter after you choose Allow statistics. Declining does not affect the calculator. Your choice is stored on this device under izzopac_measurement_v1; you can change it below the calculator. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control preferences keep optional statistics off.

When enabled, the service receives visit and product-click counts labelled with a limited channel, campaign and post number. The calculator does not send your input values, estimates, copied text, full query strings or referring page URLs. GoatCounter necessarily receives technical request information, such as your IP address and browser headers, to deliver and aggregate the service. We do not enable advertising pixels or individual-pageview collection. See GoatCounter privacy details. Counts cover consenting visitors whose browsers allow the service; they are not complete visitor totals or confirmed sales.

Measurement test visits use a separate test label. Payhip handles any product purchase under its own privacy policy. Contact: IZZOPAC SOLUTIONS.
