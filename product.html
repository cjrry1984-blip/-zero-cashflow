No. It helps you organise enquiries and prepare drafts. You remain in control of every customer message.

Is an AI subscription required?

No. The templates and tracker work without AI. AI prompts are optional and can be used with a suitable assistant.

No. It is a general organisational toolkit. Check your own privacy, retention and business obligations.
