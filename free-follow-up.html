Three customer follow-up messages you can use today.

For freelancers, cleaners, contractors and other solo service businesses. Copy, personalise and send with care. You remain in control of every message.

1. Acknowledge a new enquiry

Hi [first name], thanks for contacting [business] about [service]. I've received your message and will get back to you by [realistic time]. If there's anything important I should know beforehand, feel free to reply. Best, [your name]

2. Follow up on an estimate

Hi [first name], just checking whether you had any questions about the estimate I sent for [service] on [date]. I'm happy to clarify the scope or next steps. No pressure — let me know what works for you. Best, [your name]

3. Close the loop politely

Hi [first name], I wanted to close the loop on your enquiry about [service]. If it's still relevant, you're welcome to reply and we can pick things up. Otherwise, no action needed. Thanks for considering [business].

Replace the brackets with correct details. Contact people only when you have a legitimate reason and respect opt-outs and local requirements. Don't put confidential customer details into AI tools without considering privacy.
